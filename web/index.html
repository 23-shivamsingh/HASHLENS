METHOD & DOCUMENTATION — 003

How it works.

Three simple signals, one ranked answer. Hash identification looks at how a string is formatted, not what secret it represents.

01 /
$

Prefix recognition

Self-describing encodings often begin with recognizable markers such as $2b$, $argon2id$, or {SSHA}.

STRONG SIGNAL · HIGH CONFIDENCE
02 /
#

Length & shape

Hexadecimal length narrows the options. 32 hex characters could mean MD5, NTLM, MD4, or RIPEMD-128. Ambiguity is intentional.

POSSIBLE MATCH · MEDIUM / LOW
03 /
Aa

Character alphabet

Hex, base64-like characters, modular separators, and challenge-response fields help distinguish families and reject mismatches.

HEURISTICS · NOT CRYPTOGRAPHIC PROOF
RESPONSIBLE USE

For identification, not cracking.

HASHLENS does not attempt to recover passwords, compare against breach datasets, upload secrets, or make network requests. Never paste real tokens or sensitive production credentials, even into local tools, unless you have authorization and understand the risk.

Includes a local Python CLI for terminal workflows. The browser engine runs independently, with no install step.
